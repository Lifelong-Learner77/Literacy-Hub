💡 Study Tips

Tips for Students

Small habits make a big difference. Try one of these every day.

📅

Read Every Day

Even 15 minutes a day adds up fast. Pick something you enjoy — it counts!

📝

Write Before You Write

Use the scaffold tool to plan your ideas before you start writing your paragraph.

🔍

Look Up New Words

When you see a word you don't know, look it up and write it down. Use it in a sentence.

🗣️

Talk About What You Read

Tell a friend or family member about your reading. Explaining it out loud helps you understand it better.

❓

Ask Questions

While reading, ask yourself: Who? What? Why? How? This keeps your brain active and focused.

🔁

Re-read Confusing Parts

If something doesn't make sense, read it again slowly. It's OK to go back!

✅

Check Your Work

After writing, read your work out loud. Your ears will catch mistakes your eyes miss.

🎯

Set a Small Goal

Pick one skill to work on each week — fluency, vocabulary, or writing sentences. Small goals lead to big results.
